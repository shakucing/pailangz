# Tournament setup: staff guide

The page separates setup into four sections. On a laptop, readiness sits beside the selected task. On a phone, it sits above a two-column task selector, with the full checklist collapsed. Edit forms open in dialogs so staff can concentrate on one change. The tournament diagram can be collapsed whenever you need more space.

Click a “Ready to publish” checklist item to open its task dialog, including completed items you want to review. The dialog scrolls to the relevant field or action, focuses it, and highlights it for three seconds. The team check opens an incomplete roster for editing, or a new team form when another team is needed.

## Other page actions

**SOLO & TEAM registration links:** the first overview panel links to player entry. Use **Registration links** in the summary to reach it from any tab. Share the event-specific participation URL after enabling registration. The landing page also shows player entry and team viewing links for its featured enabled event, with the current player registration status; it does not offer team creation or management. Registration/team pages can be available while fixtures and results remain private. The deadline, capacity and event lifecycle also control whether entries are accepted.

**Team roster management:** in **Overview & schedule**, choose **Player-managed** to let approved players create teams and owners review join requests, or **Staff-managed** to have staff create teams and assign rosters in **Players & teams**. Players register individually in both modes. Changing this setting keeps existing teams, assignments and application history.

**Landing page SOLO / TEAM tabs:** the event centre switches between the separate official tournaments. Each tab shows that event's name, capacity, progression, entrants, fixtures and results. TEAM also shows team names and roster counts; available team directory links use the TEAM tournament URL. A tournament still in preparation shows a schedule preview while unpublished scores stay private. Publication and registration are controlled separately for each event.

**Confirm player list:** record that names and player codes have been reviewed. Do this again after a roster change that invalidates the confirmation.

**Publish tournament / Unpublish tournament:** use the Publication panel beside the tasks on a laptop, or above them on a phone. It stays available on every tournament tab. Unpublishing returns official fixtures and results to private staff access while retaining players and match history. Publishing still requires every readiness check; unpublishing does not. Opening registration is a separate setting; the panel's Overview & schedule link opens the registration switch.

If publication fails because the event is Draft or Archived, click the error message to open Overview & schedule with Tournament status focused and highlighted. Readiness errors also link to the same task dialogs and fields as the checklist.

**Tournament status:** existing events can move to any listed status, including reopening registration or correcting a completed/archived event. Changing status retains players, fixtures and results. Draft and Archived unpublish the event and its active stages; returning to an active status requires a separate Publish action to make it public again. Every status correction is recorded in activity history.

**Edit tournament rules:** configure the rules for the selected stage, then save. Saving automatically confirms each configured rule; there are no separate confirmation checkboxes. Blank rules remain undecided, and clearing a saved choice removes its confirmation. “Rules update” is that stage's rules revision. The confirmed-rule summary shows the saved choices.

**Manage stage progression:** save final rankings for league stages, create qualification fixtures, or create a confirmed SOLO/TEAM bracket as applicable. Click a player's name in the rankings, playoff player list, or manual SOLO starting order to review their match results. Closing the results returns to progression with any unsaved ranking changes intact. Resolve ties using the confirmed rules; these controls do not automatically advance an unfinished competition.

Stage cards follow League → Qualification → Knockout, with TEAM progression shown separately. The first unfinished stage in each category is highlighted as **Current stage**; later stages are **Upcoming**. League and qualification are **Completed** once valid final rankings are saved. Knockout is **Completed** once every match is settled and the final has an accepted decisive result. Stale dependencies keep a stage unfinished.

For playoffs, **Edit tournament rules → Playoff opponents** offers **Staff choose each pairing** and **Auto assign balanced opponents**. After saving the automatic rule and freezing the league ranking, use **Manage stage progression → Auto assign & create fixtures**. Every playoff entrant receives the configured number of matches against distinct opponents. Existing playoff fixtures cannot be overwritten.

**Player eligibility:** in the assigned-player list, click **Eligible** or **Ineligible** to switch that player's eligibility immediately. The button shows the saved state; no edit dialog or separate Save action is needed. Checkboxes and the **Mark eligible / Mark ineligible** buttons still support updating several players together. Published tournaments must be unpublished before eligibility changes, and inactive/unverified members cannot be marked eligible.

**Remove player:** open **Competition → Tournament participation** and click the trash icon on the entrant's row. Alternatively, open **Tournaments & rules → choose the tournament → Players** (or **Players & teams**) **→ Review assigned players & eligibility** and use the same icon beside their name. Enter a reason and select **Remove player from tournament**. This withdraws the entrant, frees their slot and retains their community member record and tournament history. A withdrawn player needs staff to restore their place before rejoining. Published tournaments must be unpublished first; team captains need a replacement or an archived team. Review fixtures and confirm the player list after removal.

**Withdraw or replace a player:** use this separate action on the tournament's Players tab when transferring a place to a replacement. Unplayed draft fixtures may be rebuilt and require schedule/player-list review. Started competition or saved scores require an admin-controlled restart.

**Show retained stage/result history:** inspect archived stages from earlier revisions. The link now retains the selected tournament filter.

## Suggested staff workflow

In **Tournaments & rules → Landing page highlights**, choose one SOLO tournament, one TEAM tournament, both, or **None** for either format, then select **Save landing highlights**. A format with no selection is hidden from the landing page event centre; clearing both hides the entire section. Each format has a single selection, so highlighting a different tournament replaces the previous choice. Admins and moderators can manage these selections. Unpublished selections expose only a schedule preview; official results still require publication. Archived tournaments are hidden and cannot be selected. New installations start with no highlights.

1. Set the game, dates and descriptions in Overview & schedule; enable registration when ready.
2. Approve membership and participation, assign any remaining players, and complete team rosters.
3. Confirm each stage's rules and generate/check its fixtures.
4. Confirm the player list and finish the readiness checklist, then publish.
5. Use Fixtures & results during matches; return to Rules & stages when final rankings or a new bracket need confirmation.

For future refinement, a single “next task” based on the current competition stage could guide staff toward scoring during live play and setup during drafts. The current change keeps the existing competition and permission rules while making each task easier to find.

## Series lengths and a separate final

In **Create tournament** or **Sizes & updates → Change tournament sizes**, open **Series lengths**. Set **SOLO knockout best of** or **TEAM knockout best of** to `5`, then set the corresponding **final best of** to `7`. Quarterfinals, semifinals and earlier knockout rounds will use BO5; only the final will use BO7. SOLO league length has its own input, and playoff length is set in the qualification rules. All series lengths must be odd whole numbers from 1 to 99. BO7 finishes at four game wins. Existing events keep their previous defaults until changed through the usual configuration revision workflow; revisions preserve earlier fixtures and results in history.
